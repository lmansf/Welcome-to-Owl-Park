A small, safe chatroom world

Owl Park is a small, safe chatroom world: no typed chat, no downloads, one park. You visit it in your browser as an owl, alongside everyone else who is here right now.

Make your owl

What a chatroom world is

A place you visit as a character, where you see everyone else who is there at the same time. You walk around, say hello, play small games, and make a corner of it your own.

What Owl Park keeps from the old ones

Built for anyone who misses the old chatroom worlds: grown-up owls welcome.

You wander the park as an owl with everyone who is in it at the same time. There are quests from the park’s owls, quick arcade games, fishing, the stage quiz and leaf piles to jump in, and a nest of your own to decorate and open to visitors.

What it does differently

  • No typed chat. Owls talk only with the 24 set phrases on the Say wheel, so nobody can type a message to anyone in the park.
  • No download and no account. The park opens in your browser, on a computer or a phone, and the owl lives in that browser.
  • Free to look around. Guests come in free and keep nothing they win; ticketed owls keep everything.
  • Nothing is sold inside the park. A Day Ticket is bought in the store’s checkout, which Shopify runs.

Next to app-based worlds

Many online worlds ask for an app download or an account before you see anything. Owl Park opens in the browser tab you already have, and guests walk in free.

Grown-ups can read how chat stays safe and what the park keeps in A note for grown-ups, and the answers to common questions in Questions.
